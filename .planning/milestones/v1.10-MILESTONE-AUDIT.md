## Summary

Milestone v1.10 achieved its definition of done. F5 natural pacing now keeps
human-like sentence and clause pauses while replacing audible breath splices
with configurable silent pauses. The live/cache path also invalidates prepared
and response cached WAVs when F5 pacing knobs change, so stale pre-fix audio
cannot silently bypass the new behavior.
